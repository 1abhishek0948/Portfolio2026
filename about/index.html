What I do

I take products from a rough idea to a reliable, working experience. On a typical project I might design the database schema, build the Django backend and REST API, develop the React frontend, and handle deployment. I care about software that stays maintainable long after launch, not just demos that look good on day one.

  • Full stack web development — Django, React, PostgreSQL and JavaScript, from database to interface. See full stack development.
  • Custom software and SaaS products — authentication, user roles, dashboards and integrations. See SaaS development.
  • APIs and databases — REST APIs, secure integrations and well-modelled data layers. See API development.
  • Python development and applied AI — automation, OpenCV computer vision and LLM integrations. See Python development.

Where I'm from

I grew up in Lahan, one of the main towns of Siraha district in Madhesh Province, southeastern Nepal, and completed my +2 in Science at Little Star Higher Secondary English School there. Being from a growing commercial town like Lahan shaped how I think about software: I like building things that ordinary people and small businesses can actually use. Today I work with clients in Lahan, Siraha and across Nepal, including Kathmandu, and remotely with teams anywhere.

Background

I'm completing a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at Parul University (2022–2026), where my coursework covers software engineering, data structures, databases, web development and machine learning fundamentals. Alongside my degree I've completed two internships: a Full Stack Developer internship at Unified Mentor, where I built AI Web Doctor with React, Django and PostgreSQL, and a Web Development internship at Prodigy InfoTech.

Read more about my education and coursework, or browse the technologies I work with.

Selected work

My portfolio includes LAHAN Hub, a digital city ecosystem of 21 interconnected Django applications; Lafzloom, a multilingual poetry platform; and a Blind Assistance System built with OpenCV and Raspberry Pi. I write about how these projects are made in the articles section.

How I work

I start with what the product needs to do for real users, keep the scope honest, and build in small verifiable steps. I prefer clear communication over jargon, and I'd rather tell you when something won't work than promise it anyway. If you have a project in mind, tell me about it.
