About Abhishek Thakur

Abhishek Thakur Barhi is a Software Engineer and Full Stack Developer from Lahan, Siraha, Madhesh Province, Nepal. He builds responsive interfaces, backend systems, authentication flows and REST APIs, with Python and Django on the backend and React on the frontend.

His work combines practical product thinking with an interest in reliable implementation. His portfolio includes LAHAN, a digital city ecosystem, Lafzloom, a multilingual poetry platform, and assistive computer-vision experiments using OpenCV and Raspberry Pi.

Education and direction

He is studying for a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at Parul University. His development interests include web application architecture, databases, APIs, frontend usability, machine learning fundamentals and computer vision.
