Website Design Services in Nepal

Good web design isn't decoration — it's how your site communicates. I design interfaces that look clean, load fast, work on every screen size, and guide visitors toward doing what they came for.

What design work covers

  • Page structure and content hierarchy — deciding what comes first, what supports it, and what stays out of the way
  • Responsive layouts — designed mobile-first, tested on real screen sizes
  • Typography and spacing — readable text and a clear visual rhythm
  • Component design — buttons, forms, cards and navigation that behave consistently
  • Accessibility basics — contrast, focus states, keyboard navigation and semantic structure

My design approach

I start from content and user goals, not templates. A restaurant site in Lahan and a poetry platform like Lafzloom need completely different design decisions, and pretending otherwise produces generic sites. I keep iterations fast: we review a structure, agree on direction, refine details, and only then polish visuals. Revisions are part of the process — within the agreed scope, not endlessly.

Design and build, together

Because I also build what I design (see web development), nothing gets lost between a design file and the final site. The design you approve is the design that ships — pixel-accurate, responsive, and performance-checked. That also avoids the classic handover problem where the designer, the developer and the SEO person each blame the others.

Where I work

Most design work happens remotely, which suits clients anywhere in Nepal — including Lahan, Siraha, Kathmandu and beyond — as well as international clients. Reviews happen over video or annotated screenshots.

Frequently asked questions

Can you design around my existing brand?

Yes. If you have a logo, colours or brand materials, I'll design within them. If you don't, I'll propose a simple, consistent visual direction that suits your business.

Do you write the content for my site?

I help structure content — what pages you need, what each page should say, in what order. For final copy, I recommend writing it yourself (you know your business best) or working with a writer; I'll review and tighten it for the web.

How many revisions do I get?

Design is iterative within the agreed scope: we refine until the direction is right, then lock details. What I avoid is unbounded revision loops — they're a sign the goals weren't clear, so we fix that instead.
