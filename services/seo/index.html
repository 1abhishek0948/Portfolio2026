Technical SEO Services in Nepal

Technical SEO is the engineering side of search visibility: making sites that search engines can crawl, understand, and serve quickly. I fix the technical foundations — and I don't sell ranking guarantees, because anyone who does is guessing with your money.

What a technical SEO engagement covers

  • Crawlability and indexing — robots.txt, XML sitemaps, canonical URLs, and removing whatever blocks search engines
  • Site architecture — clean URL structure, logical internal linking and no duplicate-content confusion
  • Metadata — unique titles and meta descriptions across every indexable page
  • Structured data — valid Schema.org markup (Organization, Service, FAQ, articles) with nothing fake
  • Core Web Vitals — real performance work: image formats, render-blocking resources, caching and JavaScript weight
  • Mobile and accessibility — the fundamentals that both users and search engines depend on

This very site is my working example: the metadata, sitemap, structured data and performance work you can inspect here are the standard I deliver.

What SEO is not

I don't offer guaranteed positions, secret tricks, or thousand-page link farms. That's not caution — it's that they don't work, or actively harm you. Rankings come from useful content on a technically sound site, over time. What I can promise: your site will be crawlable, fast, correctly structured and honestly represented to search engines. This site's own articles are my approach in practice — content written for humans first.

Frequently asked questions

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and no honest provider does. I fix the technical fundamentals and structure your site so it can compete. Content quality and competition determine the rest.

How long before SEO shows results?

Technical fixes can take effect within weeks as search engines re-crawl. Competitive positions, though, build over months of useful content and credibility. Anyone promising otherwise is selling a story.

Technical SEO or content — which do I need first?

Technical first: great content on a broken, slow, uncrawlable site underperforms what it deserves. Once the foundation is right, content compounds properly.

Can you do SEO for a business in Lahan or another town in Nepal?

Yes — local relevance done honestly: correct business details, real service-area information, and pages that carry genuinely useful local content instead of keyword-stuffed copies.
