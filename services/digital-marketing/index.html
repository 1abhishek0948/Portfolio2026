Digital Marketing & Organic Growth

Digital marketing done honestly: I help small businesses get found and understood online through organic growth — technical SEO, content structure, local visibility and measurement. I don't run ad campaigns, and I won't pretend that a Facebook page is a strategy.

What's actually in scope

  • Organic visibility — the technical SEO foundation plus page structure aimed at what your customers search for
  • Content strategy — what pages and articles your business genuinely needs, planned around real questions your customers ask
  • Local visibility — being findable for the searches that matter where you operate, with honest business details everywhere
  • Analytics and measurement — knowing where visitors come from and what they do, instead of guessing
  • Website as the hub — a fast, clear site (see web development) that everything else points to

What's out of scope — and why that's a feature

I don't manage paid ad campaigns, and I don't sell engagement-farming or follower packages. Those are separate skills, and pretending to cover everything is how businesses get mediocre versions of everything. What you get from me is the organic side done properly: a technically sound site, content structured around what people actually search for, and honest measurement of whether it's working. When you do need paid media, I'll tell you to hire someone who specialises in it.

A realistic arc

Month one: technical foundation and measurement in place. Following months: content built around real customer questions, structure refined from data. Growth compounds — slowly at first, then meaningfully. Businesses that need "viral" by Friday should talk to someone else; businesses that want to be the obvious answer when someone in their town searches for their service are in the right place. Read my guide for local businesses in Lahan for a taste of the approach.

Where I work

Small businesses in Lahan, Siraha and across Nepal are exactly who this service fits: real products, real customers, and marketing that can't afford to be wasted. Kathmandu businesses get the same approach, remotely or in person.

Frequently asked questions

Do you run Facebook or Google ads?

No. I focus on organic growth where my engineering skills genuinely add value. For paid campaigns, hire a specialist — I can help make sure the ads point to a fast, working site.

So what do you actually do for digital marketing?

Make your business findable and credible online: a technically sound website, content that answers what your customers search for, correct local information, and analytics so decisions come from data.

Can you write our content?

I structure content and write technical material well. For your core business copy, deep subject knowledge wins — I'll help shape, edit and optimise what you know, rather than fabricate expertise.

Is this a monthly retainer?

It can be ongoing, or a scoped one-time project (audit, site fixes, content plan) that you execute yourself. You get the option, not a lock-in.
