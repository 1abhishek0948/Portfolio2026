Web Development in Lahan & Nepal

I build fast, responsive, SEO-friendly websites and web applications for businesses, organisations and individuals — from a first business website in Lahan to full web platforms used across Nepal.

What I build

  • Business websites — a clear, credible home for your business with the pages, content and contact flows you actually need.
  • Web applications — dashboards, portals and tools where users log in, save data and get work done (built with Django, React and PostgreSQL).
  • Landing pages — focused pages for a product, service or campaign that load fast and convert.
  • Portfolio and profile sites — like this one, designed around your work and your name.
  • Redesigns and rebuilds — modernising an outdated site or migrating it to a maintainable stack.

What's included

  • Responsive layout that works on phones, tablets and desktops
  • Semantic, accessible HTML and clean page structure
  • On-page SEO essentials: titles, meta descriptions, sitemap and structured data where appropriate
  • Contact forms with spam protection and email delivery
  • Performance basics — optimised images, modern formats and lazy loading
  • Deployment and a walkthrough of how to manage your site

How I work

First we talk about your goals, your customers and your content — not colours. Then I propose a structure, we agree on scope, and I build in reviewable steps. You see a working site early, give feedback, and we iterate. Before launch I handle the technical checklist: responsive testing, metadata, performance and forms. After launch, support and maintenance is available if you want it.

Technologies

For content-driven sites: HTML, CSS and JavaScript with performance-first thinking. For applications: Django and PostgreSQL on the backend, React on the frontend. The choice depends on what the project needs — a five-page business site doesn't need a JavaScript framework, and a data-heavy application shouldn't be built without one.

Frequently asked questions

Do you build websites for businesses in Lahan and nearby areas?

Yes. I'm based in Lahan, Siraha, and regularly work with local businesses, schools and organisations — in person where practical. I also work with clients across Nepal and remotely.

How long does a business website take?

A small business website typically takes a few weeks once content is ready; larger web applications take longer and are estimated after we scope the work. Content — text and images — is the most common delay, so preparing it early helps.

Will my website be SEO-friendly?

Yes: semantic HTML, proper titles and descriptions, sitemap and robots configuration, fast pages and structured data where it genuinely applies. What I won't do is promise rankings — see my honest take in the technical SEO service.

Can you help with domain and hosting?

Yes. I'll help you choose and register a domain, set up hosting or a deployment platform, and connect everything — then hand over ownership properly in your name.
