Where I work

I'm based in Lahan, Siraha (Madhesh Province) and work with clients locally, across Nepal — from Kathmandu to Biratnagar and Pokhara — and remotely with teams anywhere in the world. Most communication happens over email, phone or video calls, so location rarely limits what we can build together.

A note on pricing and promises

Honest scope, honest price

There is no fixed price list here because two "simple websites" are rarely equally simple. Price follows scope, and scope follows our conversation. Similarly, I don't promise search rankings or overnight growth — I build things properly and tell you honestly what to expect.

Not sure which service fits? Describe your idea and I'll point you in the right direction — even if that direction is "you don't need custom software yet". You can also judge my work directly on the projects page or read the articles.
