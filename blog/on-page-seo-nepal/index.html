On-Page SEO for Nepali Websites: What to Fix First

The on-page fixes that move a Nepali website's ranking, in the order that pays: the title and the H1, the first paragraph, the internal links, and the one that is usually the biggest gap.

Abhishek Thakur Published Oct 27, 2026 · Updated Oct 27, 2026· 3 min read

On-page SEO is the fixes on the page that tell the search engine what it is and why it matters, and the order that pays is the cheap, high-leverage ones first. This guide is that order and the biggest gap, which is usually the internal link, not the keyword.

This is method, not a fact; the specifics fit your page.

The order that pays

The fixes are the four, and the order is the leverage, the cheap ones that are the high-leverage first:

Fix What it is Why it is first
The title and the H1 The two that say what the page is They are the found-one, and they are the cheap
The first paragraph The one that is the answer, in the natural words The one that is the snippet, and the one that is the click
The keyword, once each In the title, the H1 and the first paragraph The natural one, not the stuff
The internal links The bridges that make the page the site, not the island The one that is usually missing, and the one that is the second search

The four are the on-page, and the order is the leverage, and the leverage is the cheap, high-leverage first, and the last one is the biggest gap, because the last one is the missing bridge.

The keyword, natural, not stuffed

The keyword is the word the search is for, and it belongs once each in the title, the H1 and the first paragraph, in the place that is the natural one. The stuff is the not-natural one, and the not-natural is the not-found one, because the search engine reads the natural as the answer and the stuff as the not-answer. The natural is the one that is the found one, and the natural reads like the thing that answers, which is the whole of the on-page keyword.

The biggest gap, the internal link

The page is the island, and the island is not the site, and the internal link is the bridge that makes the page the found one for the second and the third search, not just the first. The missing bridge is the one that is usually the gap, because the page is built and the links are not, and the not-links are the island, and the island is the not-found one for the search that is the related one. The move is the two or three internal links, to the page that is the related one, and that is the bridge, and the bridge is the on-page that is the biggest gap. The external side of it, the link that is not the internal one, is earning backlinks for Nepali businesses, and the check that the page is the found one is the basic SEO audit.

A sensible default

Do the four, in the leverage order: the title and the H1 that say what the page is, the first paragraph that is the answer, the keyword once each in the natural place, and the two or three internal links that make the page the site. The four are the on-page that pays, and the last one is the gap, and the gap is the one that is the found one for the second and the third search.

Bottom line: on-page is the four, the title and H1, the first paragraph, the keyword once each, and the internal links, in the leverage order, and the biggest gap is the internal link. Do the four and the page is the found one for the second and the third search, not just the first, which is the whole of the on-page.

FAQ

Which on-page fix should I do first?

The title and the H1, because they are the two that tell the search engine what the page is, and the one that is off is the one that is not found. The title and the H1 are the first, and they are the cheap, high-leverage ones, ahead of the rest.

Do I need to stuff keywords into the page?

No. The keyword is the word the search is for, and it belongs in the title, the H1 and the first paragraph, once each, in the natural place. The stuff is the not-natural one, and the natural one is the one that is the found one, because the natural reads like the answer.

What is the biggest on-page gap most Nepali sites have?

The internal links, because the page is the island, and the island is not the site, and the internal link is the bridge, and the bridge is the one that is usually missing, and the missing bridge is the page that is not the found one for the second and the third search.
