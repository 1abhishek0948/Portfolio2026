FAQ

What is the real difference between a static site and a CMS?

Who changes it and how. The CMS is the one the person changes without the developer, and the static one is the one the developer changes, and the difference is the speed of the change, not the quality of the page, which is why the choice is the who and the how often.

Which one is cheaper, really?

The static one is cheaper to run, because it is the fewer moving parts and the less to update, and the CMS is cheaper to change, because the change is not the developer. The cost is the two sides of the same coin, and the one you pay is the one that is the who.

When is the CMS the right one?

When the site is the one that changes a lot, and the one who changes it is not the developer, and the two are the CMS, and the one that is not them is the static one, and the move is the who and the how often, not the taste of the tool.
