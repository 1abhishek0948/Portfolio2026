The open marketplaces are where a developer starts; the specialist and top-talent platforms are where the same developer raises the ceiling. The difference is the gate, and the gate is a feature, not a barrier, because it is what lets the rates go up. This guide is how the two lanes differ and the order to run them.

Platform rules, the vetting and the rates change, so confirm the current ones. This is practical guidance, not a guarantee of income.

## Why the gate is the point

| Lane | How you get work | The rate |
|---|---|---|
| Open marketplace | You apply, you compete, you earn the review | Set by the crowd |
| Specialist platform | You are vetted in, the work is routed to you | Set by the depth you show |

The second lane pays more because the first mile of trust is already paid for, by the gate. The seller who clears the gate is not competing for the next buyer, the buyer is choosing from the cleared set, and that is the whole difference in the number.

## What it actually takes to get in

The bar is specific and evidenced, not a general "be excellent." It is: one area you are clearly good at, a small set of proof that shows it in that area, and the confidence to put the two together for a review. The move is to sharpen the one area and gather the two or three pieces of proof, which is the same portfolio work from [building a portfolio that wins clients](/blog/freelancing-portfolio/), aimed at the specialist gate instead of the open one.

## The order to run the two lanes

Run them in the order that builds the evidence before it is needed:

1. **The open lane first**, to get the finished work and the reviews that prove you ship.
2. **The specialist lane second**, with that evidence, to raise the ceiling on the depth you have shown.

Reversing the order is the mistake: applying to the gate with no finished, public work is the application that is not shortlisted, because the gate reads the evidence, not the ambition.

## The habit that keeps the ceiling up

On a specialist platform, the income is the depth you maintain, not the one time you got in. The habit is to keep the one area sharp and the proof current, because the gate that got you in is the same standard that keeps the rates up. A developer who stops sharpening the area quietly slides back to the open-market number, even on the specialist platform.

## A sensible default

Use the open marketplace to build the finished, public evidence, then apply to the specialist gate with it, and keep the one area sharp so the ceiling holds. That is the two-lane income, and it is the difference between a busy account and a high one. The wider platform picture is in [top freelance platforms that work in Nepal](/blog/top-freelance-platforms-nepal/).

**Bottom line:** the specialist lane pays for depth because the gate already paid the first mile of trust. Build the evidence on the open lane, clear the gate with it, and keep the one area sharp, and the platform is a ceiling raiser instead of another crowded room.
