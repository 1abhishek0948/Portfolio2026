The website that is not maintained is not the one that is old; it is the one that quietly becomes the broken one, the slow one, the one with the hole, on a day that is not planned. This guide is what the maintenance is, why it is the habit and not the project, and how to think about the retainer so the site is not that broken one.

This is judgment about your site; the specifics are the shape, not a price.

## What the maintenance actually is

The maintenance is the four, on a schedule, and they are the cheap ones that are the site staying the site:

| Piece | What it is | The skip that hurts |
|---|---|---|
| The update | The software and the parts, current | The hole that is known and patched, on the site that is not |
| The check | The site works, the forms work, the link works | The day the customer finds the broken one |
| The small fix | The thing that is off, fixed before it is the thing | The small that becomes the large |
| The content | The page that says the true thing, updated | The page that says the old thing |

The four are the whole of it, and they are the habit, because the habit is the schedule, and the schedule is the difference between the site that is kept and the one that is skipped, and the skip is the broken one.

## Why it is the habit, not the project

The project is the version that waits for the day, and the day is the broken site, the slow site, the site with the hole, and the cost of the day is the large, uncertain one, and it is the one the project pays. The habit is the version that is the small, certain number, every month, and it is the price of the site not being the day, and that is the comparison, and the habit wins it, because the small certain is cheaper than the large uncertain, which is the whole of the maintenance case.

## The retainer, thought about right

The retainer is the price of the site not being the broken one, for the month, and it is the small, certain number, and the alternative is not free, it is the large, uncertain number of the day the site stops, and the retainer is the comparison for that. The retainer is not the cost of the site, it is the cost of the site staying the site, and the one that is skipped is the one that is the broken day, and that is the version the retainer exists to avoid. The shape of it, and the security it carries, is the subject of [the website security checklist](/blog/website-security-checklist/), and the moment the maintenance says the site is the old one, it is [when to redesign your website](/blog/when-to-redesign-website/).

## A sensible default

Put the four on a schedule, the update, the check, the small fix and the content, and let the retainer be the price of the site not being the broken one. The habit is the cheap version of the expensive day, and the retainer is the small certain number for it, and doing the four is the site that is kept, not the one that is the broken one.

**Bottom line:** the maintenance is the four, the update, the check, the small fix and the content, on a schedule, and it is the habit that is the cheap version of the broken day. Let the retainer be the price of the site not being that day, and the site is the kept one, not the quietly broken one.
