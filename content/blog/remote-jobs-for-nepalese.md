The popular list of "remote job boards" is not the list that works from Nepal, because it is not built for someone who is not in the hiring country's time zone or payroll system. The list that works is shorter, and it is split between the global boards that genuinely hire across borders and the direct channels most applicants never use. This guide is both, and the order to run them.

What is posted and who hires changes; confirm the current openings. This is practical guidance, not a job offer.

## The two lists, and why the short one wins

| List | What it is | Why it wins from Nepal |
|---|---|---|
| The global boards | The names everyone knows | Real, but a thousand applicants to each |
| The direct channels | The company, the person, the network | Fewer applicants, a faster answer |

The direct channel is where a Nepali applicant is at their strongest, because the pool is small and the work speaks for it. Running only the global boards is the race you cannot win on volume, and running the direct channel is the one you can win on the work.

## Ten sources, and what each is for

The working set is about ten, not a hundred, and each has a job:

- **The global remote boards** (a handful that hire and pay across borders) for the volume.
- **The company career pages** of the places you would actually work at, for the direct route.
- **Two or three specialist communities** where the work is shared, for the reputation lane.
- **Your own page** that the direct reach lands on, for the proof.
- **The people you know, and the people they know**, for the quiet lane that most applicants do not count.
- **The open-source and the public work**, for the evidence that the direct channel reads.

The last three are the overlooked ones, and they are where a Nepali applicant is ahead, because they reward the work and the record, not the time zone and the local resume.

## The obstacle that is not the work

The practical wall for a Nepali remote hire is the payment, not the skill: a job that cannot reach your account cleanly is a job you will fight to keep. Confirm the payout path fits your setup before you invest in the application, and that setup is the subject of [how freelancers in Nepal get paid](/blog/how-freelancers-nepal-get-paid/). The interview that follows a shortlist is in [technical interview prep for Nepali developers](/blog/technical-interview-prep/).

## The order to run it

Reach the direct channels with your page and your record, let the global boards run in the background for volume, and keep the community reputation live so the direct channel has something to land on. Run the short list, not the long one, and the remote work comes from the lane that is actually winnable.

**Bottom line:** the working list from Nepal is short: a few global boards for volume, the company pages and the direct channels for the winnable lane, and your own record for the proof. Confirm the payout fits before you apply, and run the direct channel first, because that is where a Nepali applicant is strongest.
