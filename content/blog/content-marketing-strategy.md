Content marketing fails when it is the output, not the answer, and the difference is the question it starts from. This strategy is the one question, the three pieces, and the habit that keeps it the answer, and the three are the whole of it.

This is method, not a fact; the specifics are yours.

## The one question, and why it is the strategy

The strategy is the one question the customer asks before the buy, in the customer's words, and the question is the customer's, not the business's, because the business's question is the claim and the customer's is the doubt. The strategy is the answer to the doubt, in the customer's words, and the answer is the piece, and the piece is the useful one, not the one that is the output.

## The three pieces that start it

The start is the three, and the three are the question, the proof and the move:

| Piece | What it is | Why it is the three |
|---|---|---|
| The answer | The one question, answered in the customer's words | It is the piece that is the useful |
| The proof | The thing that says the answer is true | It is the why that is the trust |
| The move | The next step that is the action | It is the one that is the conversion |

The three are the start, and the hundred that is the output without the three is the newsletter, and the three are the strategy, and the strategy is the one that is the answer, not the output.

## The habit that keeps it the answer

The habit is the question first, every piece, and the question is the customer's, and the one that is the newsletter is the one that is the output first, and the output first is the one that is the nobody reads. The move is the question first, and the question is the customer's doubt, and the doubt is the one that is the answer, and the answer is the strategy. ## A sensible default

Pick the one question the customer asks before the buy, answer it in their words, prove it, and make the move the action. The three are the strategy, and the habit is the question first, and the question first is the one that is the read one, not the newsletter.

**Bottom line:** the content strategy is the one customer question, the three pieces that are the answer, the proof and the move, and the habit of the question first. Do the three and the habit, and the content is the answer the customer reads, not the output no one does.
